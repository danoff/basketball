Model caveats

This is an exploratory model trained on one 2025 rookie sample of 41 players. DBPM is a noisy outcome shaped by team context, role, and playing time. The model explains ~26% of variance (adj. R² = 17.6%) with an RMSE of 1.49 DBPM points. No predictor reaches p < 0.05 — Shuttle_Run is marginal at p = 0.062. Treat projections as a conversation starter, not a final grade.
