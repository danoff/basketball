Use with care

Main caveats

This is an exploratory model, not a scouting grade. It was trained on one 2025 rookie sample with 41 players. DBPM is a noisy rookie-season outcome influenced by team context, role, minutes, and opportunity. The model should be used as a conversation starter, not a final projection.
